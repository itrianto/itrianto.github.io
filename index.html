01 / About me

Geospatial
professional.

Geospatial professional with hands on experience in spatial data management, digital mapping, and GIS based project support within a government/BUMN infrastructure project (PLN floating solar power plant, 100 MWac). Skilled in processing spatial data in GeoJSON, SHP, KML, and Excel/CSV formats, working with GPS coordinates and projection systems, and managing spatial databases (PostgreSQL/PostGIS, basic). Familiar with Leaflet for WebGIS visualization. Brings distinct domain strength in marine and coastal/fisheries related geospatial data from hydrographic survey and coral reef conservation project experience. Comfortable coordinating between technical and operational teams, with a track record of cross disciplinary collaboration in field and research settings.
