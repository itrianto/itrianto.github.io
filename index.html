01 / ABOUT ME

Geospatial
professional.

My journey in geospatial work has taken me across different environments, industries, and project disciplines. From land and infrastructure mapping to marine surveys, coastal conservation, and environmental monitoring, each experience has shaped how I approach spatial challenges.

I have worked across both field and office environments, combining data collection, spatial processing, quality control, analysis, and visualization. Beyond producing maps, I value the process behind them: understanding the context, ensuring data reliability, and translating technical information into something meaningful and useful.

I believe that every geospatial project offers a different perspective, and I enjoy exploring how spatial technology can contribute to a better understanding of the world around us.
