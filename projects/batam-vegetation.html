03 / Methodology

Methodology.

Annual Sentinel-2 processing chain built in Google Earth Engine.

  1. 01Sentinel-2Annual image collection, 10 m resolution.
  2. 02Cloud / water maskingMask clouds, cloud shadows, and water bodies.
  3. 03Annual compositeMedian composite for a consistent yearly scene.
  4. 04NDVINormalized Difference Vegetation Index.
  5. 05Change detectionMulti-year differencing of NDVI composites.
  6. 06StatisticsArea and magnitude of detected change.
  7. 07Land-cover contextDynamic World for classification context.
  8. 08Time seriesAnnual NDVI trend for the study area.

08 / Limitations

Limitations.

Caveats and scope constraints of the analysis.

  • BoundaryAnalysis is clipped to the FAO GAUL 2015 boundary of Batam City.
  • NDVI decreaseAn NDVI decrease is not automatically classified as deforestation; it only indicates reduced vegetation signal.
  • ThresholdChange detection applies an analytical threshold of ±0.05 in NDVI difference.
  • Dynamic WorldLand-cover transitions are indicative and carry classification uncertainty.
  • ValidationNo field verification has been carried out for this study area.
