# Projects

## 1. Religious Tourism Connectivity, Semarang

**Status:** implemented interactive research WebGIS; project card and gallery integrated on portfolio home page.

**Evidence:** thesis, GeoImage publication, GeoJSON layers, CSV attributes, and the Religious Tourism gallery (22 images).

**Challenge/objective:** analyse distribution, road-network connectivity, and visitor counts for officially recorded religious tourism sites in Semarang.

**Methodology/technology:** Avenza Map coordinate plotting; GIS spatial and network analysis; graph theory; alpha, beta, gamma connectivity indices; Leaflet and GeoJSON presentation.

**Result:** 22 sites represented against 607 nodes, 621 road edges, and a route layer. Results and caveats are documented in [07-webgis.md](07-webgis.md).
