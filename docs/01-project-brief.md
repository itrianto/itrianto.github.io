# Project Brief

## Purpose

This is Irvan Trianto's professional GIS portfolio, not a generic portfolio template. Its purpose is to communicate evidence-backed geospatial capability across GIS, hydrography, environmental mapping, spatial analysis, WebGIS, and research.

## Audiences

Primary audiences are GIS recruiters, GIS engineers, hydrographers, environmental consultants, marine companies, renewable-energy companies, and HSE recruiters. Secondary audiences are researchers, clients, universities, and students.

## Current product scope

The home page introduces the practitioner, core expertise, education, navigation anchors, contact paths, a CV download, and a link to the Religious Tourism WebGIS. The latter is the strongest implemented case study. See [06-projects.md](06-projects.md).

## Success criteria

- Claims can be traced to the CV, thesis, supplied datasets, or user-provided material.
- A recruiter can reach the profile, evidence, projects, and contact path quickly.
- The Religious Tourism page demonstrates analysis through layers, network data, filters, and research interpretation - not points alone.
- The interface remains fast, keyboard usable, responsive, and visually coherent.

## Constraints

Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript, Leaflet, GeoJSON, and GitHub Pages are the approved stack. See [AI_CONTEXT.md](AI_CONTEXT.md) and [07-webgis.md](07-webgis.md).
